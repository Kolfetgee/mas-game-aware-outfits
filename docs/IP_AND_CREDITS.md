## Distribution rules for this repository

- Do not upload official DDLC or MAS assets, music, dialogue, or packaged game files.
- Do not upload graphics, sprites, or textures from games or third-party spritepacks without suitable redistribution permission.
- Do not publish a complete game or imply this submod replaces or is endorsed by MAS/DDLC.
- Keep fan works free and label them clearly as unofficial.
- Before a playable public release, assess Team Salvato's current first-run fan-game notice requirements: disclaimed affiliation, recommendation to complete the original DDLC, and a link to https://ddlc.moe/.
- Team Salvato's IP Guidelines were updated on April 29, 2026, including restrictions on generative-AI depictions of DDLC characters in text, images, and audio. Any in-character AI-assisted dialogue in the internal prototype must be reviewed/replaced before source or a public build is distributed.
- Do not publish internal video or logs without checking for personal data, paths, or third-party artwork.

This document describes project hygiene, not legal advice. Any future license must cover only source code the project has the right to license, and must not imply rights over third-party material.
